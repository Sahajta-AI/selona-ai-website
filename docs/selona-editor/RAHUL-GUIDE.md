# Changing the Selona website yourself

**Open the editor.** For now, while you try it out, go to **selona-ai-website.vercel.app/editor** (a private copy of your website; changes there don't touch selona.ai). Once you're happy, the same editor moves to **selona.ai/editor**. Bookmark it. It isn't linked from the website.

**Sign in.** Type your email (rahul@selona.ai) and the personal access key Pranamya sent you, then press **Sign in**. (Later you'll simply press **Sign in with Google**.) You stay signed in for 30 days on that computer, so you only sign in once.

**Change words.** You see your real website. Click any words and type. Press Enter or click somewhere else when you're done. Numbers are just words too: change "10" to "12" the same way. Use the menu at the top left to go to another page (Pricing, Contact and so on).

**Words you can't click.** Some words only show sometimes, such as the page title in the browser tab or the "Thanks" message after someone sends the form. Find them on the right under **This page → Words you can't click on**.

**Pictures.** Click a picture to replace it, or use **This page → Pictures and videos → Replace**. Pictures are made smaller for you automatically.

**Lists** (use cases, questions, price plan features…). Click an item, then on the right choose **Move up**, **Move down**, **Add another** (a copy appears for you to retype) or **Remove**.

**Made a mistake?** Press **Undo** (or Ctrl+Z, ⌘Z on a Mac). **Redo** brings it back.

**See it first.** Press **Preview** to see the whole page as visitors will. **Back to editing** returns.

**Publish.** Nothing changes on the real website until you press **Publish**. Wait for **Live now ✓**: that means visitors now see your change. The **Changes** tab on the right lists everything you changed, each with its own **Undo this change**.

**If someone else changed it too**, you'll see a yellow message. Choose **See their version first** if you're unsure.

**History.** The **History** tab shows every version that went live, with the date, time and who published it. **Restore as draft** brings an old version back so you can look at it, then press Publish. Published something by mistake? Press **Undo this publish** on the top entry: the website goes straight back to how it was just before, in one click.

**Saved versions.** In **Saved versions**, type a name (for example "Before the investor round") and press **Save**. This keeps a copy of the whole website. **Bring back this version** puts it all back in one step. "Original site, before the editor (25 Nov 2026)" is always there. Nothing is ever deleted.

**From Claude.** In Claude, turn on the **Selona website** connector and just say what you want. Claude can do everything you can do in the editor: change words, pictures and lists, undo changes, publish, undo a publish, save a version and bring one back.
1. Ask for the change, for example: "On the pricing page, change 'Discuss a pilot' to 'Start a pilot'." You can also take a screenshot of part of the website, add it to the chat and say "change this heading to …".
2. Claude saves it as a draft and gives you a **preview link**. Open it: you see the old and new words side by side, already signed in, even on another computer or phone (the link works for 24 hours).
3. Happy with it? Tell Claude "publish it". Claude puts it live and tells you. (You can also press **Publish** yourself in the editor.) Not happy? Say "undo that" or ask for a different change.

Claude only publishes the changes you previewed. If someone changed something in between, Claude shows you the new list first and asks again. In **History**, everything done with Claude is marked **Done with Claude**.

To put a new picture in through Claude, give it the picture's web address (or upload it in the editor yourself). Keep your connector address private, like a password.
